## Method

**Volatility regimes** are the `regime` labels already carried by the persisted ledgers, produced by a threshold model fitted on each fold's training slice only. They are causal by construction.

**Trend regimes** come from `eda.regimes.tag_trend_volatility_regimes`, of which only the `trend_regime` output is used: price against its own trailing moving average, which looks strictly backwards. That function's volatility buckets use full-sample quantiles and are deliberately **not** used, because a slice defined with future information must never inform a candidate for the holdout.

Cells with fewer than 500 bars are reported but excluded from the corrected family: too small to support a claim either way.

**The block is corrected within itself.** Conditioning multiplies the tests, and correcting inside the block is what stops the slicing from producing the discovery it was designed to look for. This count is kept separate from the study-level count, which answered a different question.
