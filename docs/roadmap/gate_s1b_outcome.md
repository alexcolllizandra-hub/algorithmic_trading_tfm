## 2. The only verdict S1-B is allowed to reach

Section 3 of the pre-specification: S1-B drops a family "only for a mechanical
reason — no valid candidates, no trades, or a failed invariant — never for weak
performance".

**All four families are mechanically viable.** Every family produced feasible
candidates, traded in essentially every outer fold, and satisfied the freezing
invariant (each fold's winner selected on validation only and fingerprinted
before its test slice was scored). One genetic-algorithm fold of
`mtf_trend_consensus` produced no feasible candidate; that is a recorded outcome,
not an invariant breach.

By the frozen contract, all four therefore proceed to S1-C.

## 3. Development-period description (not a decision)

Reproduced from the generated report so this record stands alone. **These numbers
gate nothing.** At one seed, one asset and 25 evaluations per fold they cannot
evaluate promotion criteria C1–C6, which are defined over 10 seeds and both
assets in S1-C.

Confirmatory engine (Random Search), development out-of-sample:

| Family | Total compounded return | Median per-fold return | Median per-fold Sharpe | Folds with return > 0 | Median deflated Sharpe | Folds with DSR > 0.95 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `funding_reversal` | −38.3 % | +0.21 % | +0.17 | 8/15 | 0.576 | 0/15 |
| `mtf_trend_consensus` | −67.8 % | −6.84 % | −0.94 | 6/15 | 0.411 | 0/15 |
| `intraday_seasonality` | −42.0 % | −2.96 % | −1.22 | 4/15 | 0.091 | 0/15 |
| `xasset_spread_reversion` | −84.0 % | −8.07 % | −1.12 | 3/15 | 0.258 | 0/15 |

`funding_reversal` is the only family with a positive *median* per-fold return,
and it still compounds to −38 %: its positive folds are smaller than its negative
ones. A median is not a P&L.

Multiple-testing accounting across the four families (Random Search, benchmark =
flat, 32 385 aligned out-of-sample bars):

* Hansen SPA: statistic 0.000, p = 1.000.
* White Reality Check: statistic −0.002, p = 0.996.
* Benjamini–Hochberg at α = 0.05 over the four one-sided bootstrap p-values
  (0.75, 0.84, 0.92, 0.99): **no rejections**.
* PBO (CSCV): **not computable** from these artifacts. CSCV needs one performance
  matrix of the same configurations across all time blocks, and ADR 0012 makes
  the search independent per outer fold, so no configuration is shared between
  folds. Computing it requires evaluating a common candidate set across every
  fold, which is an S1-C task.

The deflated Sharpe uses Gaussian skewness and kurtosis, which *flatters* the
result — real crypto returns are fat tailed and accounting for that would only
lower it. An optimistic assumption cannot manufacture a negative finding.

## 4. Which branch was taken, and which was refused

Two follow-on branches were considered.

**Refused — declaring `S1 CLOSED_NEGATIVE`.** The development numbers above are
uniformly discouraging, and it is tempting to close S1 now. That would be
invalid, for two independent reasons:

1. Section 3 forbids dropping a family at S1-B for weak performance. Closing the
   whole gate on S1-B evidence is that same move applied to all four at once.
2. Section 4 defines `CLOSED_NEGATIVE` as the outcome of the S1-C promotion
   evaluation — Random Search, 10 seeds, both assets, budget 100. A verdict
   reached on one seed, one asset and a quarter of the budget is not that
   evaluation, and substituting it would change a frozen criterion after seeing
   results, which section 5 forbids.

No closure ADR was written and no gate status was changed.

**Refused — wiring the "primary vs primary + meta-labeling" comparison.** Its
trigger was at least one family showing the primary criterion (C1, positive total
net out-of-sample return) green. On the confirmatory engine, **all four families
have a negative total compounded return**, so no family met the trigger. A
meta-label filters a primary signal; it does not create one, and attaching it to
a family with no positive signal to filter would be an experiment designed to
produce a number rather than to answer a question. The machinery itself is
implemented and unit-tested (`src/perp_lab/labeling/`,
`src/perp_lab/meta_labeling/`) and is ready the moment a family qualifies.

**Taken — preparing S1-C.** `scripts/make_s1c_configs.py` generates
`configs/search_s1c_<family>.yaml` for all four families at the R3 budget of 100
per fold and engine, so an S1 family and an R3 family are compared at the same
search intensity. The generator does not read the S1-B results; which families it
covers follows from the viability screen alone, exactly as the R3 generator
ignored its own pilot metrics.

**Nothing in S1-C was executed.**

## 5. Decisions left to a human

1. **Start S1-C, or not.** The frozen contract says all four viable families
   proceed. Spending that budget on four families whose pilots all lost money is
   a cost decision, not a scientific one, and it is not automatable: stopping
   early would need a new, pre-specified batch, because the current batch
   contains no early-stopping rule.
2. **If S1-C runs**, add the common-candidate evaluation that makes PBO (CSCV)
   computable, since Addition S1-a of the pre-specification requires a PBO
   estimate and the current per-fold-independent protocol cannot supply one.
3. **Install the optional ML extras** (`uv add --optional ml lightgbm shap`,
   requires network) if the LightGBM and SHAP backends of the meta-label layer
   are to be exercised. The scikit-learn backends already work.
4. **The frozen holdout stays closed.** Nothing in S1-B touched it and nothing
   here justifies opening it.

---

## 6. Resolution (2026-08-11)

Decisions 1 and 2 were answered by a human: **S1-C is not run**, on resource
grounds, and Gate S1 is recorded as `S1_STOPPED_AFTER_PILOT_INCONCLUSIVE`. The
four families stay `PILOTED`, not `REJECTED`; Gate S1 is not `CLOSED_NEGATIVE`;
no meta-label comparison is attached; the holdout stays closed; Gate R4 stays
`SKIPPED`. Decision 3 (the optional ML extras) was carried out under Gate S2.
The S1-C configurations are retained unexecuted.

See [ADR 0017](../decisions/0017-gate-s1-stopped-after-pilot.md). The frozen
pre-specification was not edited.
