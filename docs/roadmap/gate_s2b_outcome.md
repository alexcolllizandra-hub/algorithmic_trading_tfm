### PBO by common candidate set

The gap S1-B had to leave open is now closed. For each family × asset, 120 unique
valid configurations were drawn from the frozen space with seed 2026,
backtested once each with the frozen costs and funding, and their per-block
Sharpe ratios assembled into an 8 × 120 CSCV matrix
(`src/perp_lab/reporting/s2_common_candidates.py`).

| Family | Asset | PBO | Median block Sharpe |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| `taker_flow_extreme` | BTCUSDT | 0.029 | −0.57 |
| `illiquidity_reversion` | BTCUSDT | 0.057 | −0.93 |
| `flow_price_divergence` | BTCUSDT | 0.600 | +0.00 |
| `taker_flow_extreme` | ETHUSDT | 0.286 | −0.38 |
| `illiquidity_reversion` | ETHUSDT | 0.300 | −0.77 |
| `flow_price_divergence` | ETHUSDT | 0.357 | +0.00 |

**These low PBO values are not good news, and must not be read as such.** PBO
estimates how often the configuration that looked best in one block lands below
the median in the complementary block. When the whole candidate set is reliably
unprofitable — median block Sharpe between −0.93 and 0.00 — the ranking is stable
precisely *because* the configurations are consistently bad. A low PBO on a
losing family says the search is not being fooled by noise; it says nothing about
there being anything to find. The only honest joint reading of the two tables is:
the families lose money, and they lose it consistently.

`flow_price_divergence` has a median block Sharpe of exactly zero because a third
of its (configuration, block) cells contain no position at all: 325 of 960 blocks
on BTCUSDT and 314 of 960 on ETHUSDT never move, at a median of 95 and 120 trades
per configuration over the whole scored window. Its PBO is therefore ranking
mostly-idle configurations against each other, and values of 0.60 and 0.36 are
what near-arbitrary rankings look like.

### One recorded deviation from the frozen PBO design

The frozen design says the common candidates are evaluated "over the **whole**
development partition". They are backtested over the whole partition, but block
Sharpe ratios are measured only from 2021-12-26 onward — 35 205 of 52 608
development bars.

The reason is causality, not convenience. The frozen search space includes an
optional regime gate, so the candidates cannot be scored without a regime label,
and a label fitted over the whole partition would have been estimated partly from
each bar's own future. The threshold regime model is therefore fitted on the
first walk-forward training window only (`[2020-01-01, 2021-12-26)`) and applied
forward; the bars inside that window warm the rolling statistics up and earn
nothing. The scored span is exactly the span the walk-forward folds evaluate.
The change is conservative, identical for all 120 configurations, and does not
alter what is being ranked. It is recorded here rather than absorbed silently.

## 4. What was refused

**Refused — preparing S2-C.** Section 9 makes preparation conditional on the
partial-signal criterion, and it did not fire. Generating S2-C configurations
"since they are cheap" would convert a pre-specified trigger into a formality.

**Refused — the `primary_only` versus `primary_plus_meta_labeling` comparison.**
A meta-label filters a primary signal; it cannot create one. With no family
showing a positive primary signal on the confirmatory engine, attaching the
meta-label layer would be an experiment designed to produce a number rather than
to answer a question. The layer stays implemented and unit-tested
(`src/perp_lab/labeling/`, `src/perp_lab/meta_labeling/`) and is ready if a
family ever qualifies.

**Refused — the trade-intensity placebo.** Section 7 requires it only of a family
that reaches a promotion discussion, and explicitly not of the pilot: "a pilot
that shows no signal at all does not need a placebo to explain it".

**Refused — re-running, re-parameterising or recombining R2, R3 or S1 families.**
Invariant 4 of the batch. Nothing in this batch touched them.

**Refused — declaring anything about the holdout.** It was not loaded,
summarised, or consulted. It stays frozen.

   qualified, and nothing is promoted or rejected.
2. **Whether a fourth development consultation is the end of the honest budget.**
   Four consultations (R2, R3, S1, S2) are now recorded and no correction in this
   repository covers their cumulative burden. Each further batch makes the
   eventual holdout result weaker evidence, whatever it shows.
3. **The frozen holdout stays closed.** Nothing in S2-B touched it and nothing
   here justifies opening it.
